## Purpose of the Study

You are invited to participate in a research study evaluating **mobile interface usability and cognitive load**. The study aims to understand how interface complexity affects:

- Task completion time
- Error rates
- Mental workload
- Overall user experience

---

## What Participation Involves

If you agree to participate, you will:

1. Complete a **demographics questionnaire** (age, gender, education, technology experience)
2. Complete **3 tasks** using Interface A (simple navigation)
3. Complete a **NASA-TLX questionnaire** (workload assessment)
4. Complete **3 tasks** using Interface B (complex navigation)
5. Complete a **NASA-TLX questionnaire** (workload assessment)
6. Complete a **System Usability Scale (SUS)** questionnaire
7. **Export your data** (optional)
